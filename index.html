A little guide

♡ Walk with WASD or arrow keys.
♡ Drag the scene to rotate the camera. Scroll to zoom.
♡ Click a frame, or walk close and press E.
♡ Press H to send a floating heart.
♡ Press B or tap 🐾 Pet near Bogart for a purring animation.
♡ On phones, use the left joystick to walk and swipe the scene to look, even at the same time.
♡ Tap a wall photo to preview it, or tap VIEW near a memory.
♡ Tap outside the preview or its × button to return. SCREEN toggles fullscreen where supported.
♡ Curate opens your photo and video collection.
♡ Together connects your private two-person museum.
♡ Escape closes a panel. Bogart follows you automatically.

Local uploads are stored on this device only. Export a backup before clearing browser data. Online mode uses private storage and owner-only upload rules.
